Bode Creative is a new business consultancy and marketing solutions company, launched in Bali as a division of the Bode Group.

We offer small and mid-sized businesses something the local market rarely provides: a complete in-house marketing and operations team, available without the cost, recruitment risk or management burden of employing one directly.

Why this model exists

Bali's professional services market is well supplied with agencies that post content, and with consultants who deliver strategy documents. Far fewer take responsibility for what happens in between.

A business owner does not need twelve reels a month. They need more enquiries, converted better, at a lower cost than they are paying now. That is a different job, and it needs a different kind of team.
