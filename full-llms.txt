# Company Overview
Bode Creative is a business consultancy and marketing solutions company based in Bali, Indonesia, operating as a division of the Bode Group. The company provides social media, advertising, web, creative production and operational support to businesses seeking to become more visible, more streamlined and more profitable.

# Founder
Lisa Sibley founded Bode Creative after years of running a luxury villa portfolio in Bali. She identified a gap in the market: businesses had plenty of advice but lacked teams who would execute consistently and stay accountable for results.

# The Gap We Address
Bali's professional services market has many agencies that post content and consultants who deliver strategy documents. Few take responsibility for what happens between strategy and execution. Most businesses are not short of advice. They are short of people who will actually do the work, consistently, month after month, and stay accountable for whether it produces anything.

# Our Model
Rather than selling deliverables by the piece, Bode Creative operates as an embedded department: a marketing manager, a photographer and videographer, a web and graphics specialist and a content team, working across a client's business as their own staff would. A business owner does not need twelve reels a month. They need more enquiries, converted better, at a lower cost than they are paying now.
